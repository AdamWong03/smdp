# AI Use Disclosure — Draft for Nature Portfolio

**Status:** template; the authors must confirm the tool names, purposes, and scope before
submission.

Nature Portfolio requires a statement in the manuscript (before the references) describing any
generative AI or AI-assisted tools used in the preparation of the work. AI tools must not be listed
as authors.

## Suggested statement (edit the bracketed parts)

> During the preparation of this work, the authors used [tool name and version] for
> [language editing / code debugging / literature search / figure preparation]. After using this
> tool, the authors reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for the
> content of the publication.

If no generative AI was used, state:

> The authors did not use generative AI or AI-assisted tools in the preparation of this work.

## Notes

- Do not claim AI-generated scientific results; the authors are responsible for all data,
  analyses, and claims.
- If AI was used only for language editing, say so explicitly.
- Check the target journal's current policy at submission time; Nature Portfolio updates its
  AI-use guidance periodically.
- The manuscript currently has a placeholder section in `paper/latex/main.tex`:
  `\section*{AI use disclosure}`.
